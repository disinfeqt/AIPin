AIPin

《魔兽世界：Forever》插件：让 AI 助手把 NPC 和地点标在你的地图上，并读取你的角色信息。 A World of Warcraft: Forever addon that lets your AI assistant pin NPCs and places on your map and read your character info.

功能Features

地图标记Map pins

NPC 和地点直接标在世界地图上，可以同时显示多个标记，鼠标悬停显示名字和坐标。最新的标记还会设置游戏的导航箭头，箭头上显示标记名字。NPCs and places are pinned right on the world map, several at once; hover a pin for its name and coordinates. The newest pin also sets the game's waypoint arrow, labelled with the pin's name.

待办清单 & 地理提醒Todos & geo-reminders

添加待办，给它设一个区域，到了就提醒。只写区域名就行，比如 @ 暴风城，不用背坐标。也可以在面板里直接添加，或点「提醒在此处」记下当前位置。Add a todo, attach a zone, and get reminded when you arrive. A zone name such as @ Stormwind is enough; no coordinates needed. You can also add todos in the panel, or click 提醒在此处 (Remind here) to use your current spot.

助手推送Assistant push

能改你电脑文件的 AI 助手可以直接编辑 Inbox.lua，推送标记和待办，同步后自动导入。An AI agent that can edit files on your computer writes pins and todos into Inbox.lua; they are imported on the next sync.

角色信息Character snapshot

等级、经验、金币、位置、天赋、专业、任务进度、装备等保存在 AIPinChars，助手可以读取。打开过的专业窗口还会记下已学图样，助手可以据此推荐做什么装备。Level, XP, money, location, talents, professions, quest progress, gear and more are saved to AIPinChars for your assistant to read. Open a profession window once and its learned recipes are saved too, so your assistant can suggest what to craft.

小地图按钮Minimap button

左键打开面板（待办 / 标记两个标签页），右键同步。角标显示未完成的待办数量。Left-click opens the panel with Todos and Pins tabs; right-click syncs. A badge shows how many todos are still open.

安装Install

  1. 下载 AIPin.zip，然后完全退出游戏。Download AIPin.zip, then quit the game completely.
  2. 解压，把 AIPin 文件夹放进 _forever_/Interface/AddOns/。Unzip it and put the AIPin folder in _forever_/Interface/AddOns/.
  3. 启动游戏，在角色选择界面点「插件」，勾选 AIPin。Start the game, click AddOns on the character select screen and enable AIPin.

更新Updating

  1. 退出游戏，下载新的 AIPin.zip。Quit the game and download the new AIPin.zip.
  2. 删掉旧的 AIPin 文件夹，换成新的。Delete the old AIPin folder and put the new one in its place.

标记、待办和角色信息保存在 _forever_/WTF/Account/<账号>/SavedVariables/AIPin.lua，专业图样保存在各角色自己的目录下，替换插件文件夹都不会丢失。Pins, todos and character data are stored in _forever_/WTF/Account/<account>/SavedVariables/AIPin.lua, and recipes in each character's own folder; replacing the addon folder keeps all of them.

如果助手用 Inbox.lua 推送过标记或待办，替换前先备份旧的 Inbox.lua，换完再放回去，否则之后的推送可能不会被导入。If your assistant has pushed pins or todos through Inbox.lua, back up the old Inbox.lua before replacing the folder and copy it back afterwards; otherwise later pushes may not be imported.

用法Usage

把助手给你的指令粘贴到聊天框。坐标是地图百分比（0–100），和 Wowhead 一样；省略区域则用当前地图。按 M 查看标记。待办加上 @ 区域，进入该区域时提醒；再写上坐标，则走到附近时提醒。Paste the command from your assistant into chat. Coordinates are map percentages (0–100), the same as Wowhead; leave out the zone to use your current map. Press M to see the pin. Add @ zone to a todo to be reminded when you enter that zone, or add coordinates as well to be reminded near a spot. In-game messages are in Simplified Chinese.

给 AI 助手For AI assistants

把 AGENTS.md 交给你的 AI 助手（插件文件夹里也有一份）。要点：Give AGENTS.md to your AI assistant (a copy is also in the addon folder). In short:

  • 安装 / 更新：下载最新的 AIPin.zip，解压到 Interface/AddOns/，保留旧的 Inbox.lua。INSTALL.md 里有 macOS 和 Windows 的完整命令。Install / update: download the latest AIPin.zip, unpack it into Interface/AddOns/ and keep the old Inbox.lua. INSTALL.md has the full commands for macOS and Windows.
  • 先读 WTF/Account/*/SavedVariables/AIPin.lua 里的 AIPinChars（只读，取 updated 最新的一条）。等级、任务进度、金币、专业、装备都在里面，按玩家的实际情况给建议，不用再问；数据太旧就请玩家同步一次。Start by reading AIPinChars in WTF/Account/*/SavedVariables/AIPin.lua (read-only; use the entry with the newest updated). Level, quest progress, gold, professions and gear are all there, so advice can fit the player without asking; if it's stale, ask them to sync.
  • 回复一行 /aipin <zone> <x> <y> <title> 或 /aipin todo <text> @ <zone>，让玩家粘贴到聊天框。Reply with /aipin <zone> <x> <y> <title> or /aipin todo <text> @ <zone> for the player to paste into chat.
  • 或编辑 Interface/AddOns/AIPin/Inbox.lua：version 加 1，在 pins / todos 里写新条目，然后让玩家右键小地图按钮同步。Or edit Interface/AddOns/AIPin/Inbox.lua: bump version by 1, add new entries to pins or todos, then ask the player to right-click the minimap button to sync.
  • 问做什么装备时，读该角色目录下的 AIPinRecipes（已学图样；玩家打开过对应的专业窗口才会记录）。When asked what to craft, read AIPinRecipes in that character's folder (learned recipes; saved once the player has opened that profession's window).
